# VIDEOS (FX Before Coffee) — standing rules (user decisions, 13 Nov 2026)

- Every video delivery also includes, without being asked:
  1. a TikTok description (English, emoji bullets with the key numbers, "Big picture only. Not a trade signal, not financial advice.", hashtags incl. #fxbeforecoffee), given in the reply ready to copy;
  2. a thumbnail PNG 1080×1920 in the same FX Before Coffee style (headline, date chip, key-number cards), text kept clear of the right 130px and the bottom ~400px (TikTok UI).
- Pace: voice speed 0.95, 0.5 s pauses between phrases, longer scene holds; each scene gets a 💡 card explaining why the item matters.
- Music: lo-fi electric piano bed (claude/video/tiktok_audio_OVERNIGHT.py), not the older synth track — except Would You Rather episodes: WITH the original catchy beat (claude/video/tiktok_audio_BEAT.py: 140 BPM half-time trap/pop, 808, claps, hat rolls, A-minor pluck hook; music 0.34 ducked under the voice; 0.5 s end fade for the loop) (user decision, 14 Nov 2026; an earlier "no music" choice the same day was withdrawn by the user).
- Outro: "Follow FX Before Coffee. Not a trade signal." (no "tonight's check") — except Would You Rather episodes (see below).
- No photos of real officials or other public figures; officials appear as name/quote cards. Stock photos only if the user supplies them (stock sites are blocked in the cloud session).
- Formats so far: overnight recap (claude/video/tiktok_*_OVERNIGHT.*), all USD pairs (claude/video/tiktok_USDPAIRS_*), week ahead (claude/video/tiktok_WEEKAHEAD_*, 14 Nov 2026), FX 101 educational (claude/video/tiktok_EDU_FUNDAMENTALS_*, 14 Nov 2026), Would You Rather (claude/video/tiktok_WYR_* = 3-question episode; claude/video/tiktok_WYR_AAPLMSFT_* = single-question viral episode with a race animation; claude/video/tiktok_WYR_gen.py + tiktok_WYR_specs_batch1.py = generator that builds any single-question episode from a spec, kinds "race" and "cards", 14 Nov 2026 — use it for new episodes).
- Would You Rather format (user ideas, 14 Nov 2026): topics do not have to relate to the bias engine — generalist trading/investing (stocks, technical analysis, crypto, risk math). Every episode OPENS with a big animated "WOULD YOU / RATHER?" title (words pop in one by one with hit sounds, "TRADING EDITION" chip) and the voice saying "Would you rather…"; then context, A/B cards, the voice line "Need more time? Pause the video and comment your answer." with an on-screen "⏸️ Need more time? Pause & comment your answer 👇" box, an on-screen 3-second countdown with "💬 Comment A or B", then what happened with both options. NO follow outro (user decision, 14 Nov 2026): the episode ends only with a big animated "NOW THAT / YOU KNOW…" (voice: "Now that you know…", small "Not a trade signal." text), which loops straight into the opening "Would you rather…". Viral version: one question, ~40 s, suspense reveal (e.g. both values racing, loser stops first). Outcome questions use only verified, dated prices from opened pages (state adjustments, e.g. splits and dividends); educational questions explain how each option behaves, no invented win rates.
