6Contradictions requiring investigation

C1 · CAD — scored −2, realised rank 2 / 1 / 2 (1w / 2w / 4w)

H1 observation: USDCAD −1.00% (1w), −1.79% (2w); CAD positive on 4 of 5 days; CADCHF near its two-month high.

Explanations checked: (a) BoC pricing for 28 Oct rose from ~30% to ~80% on 17–20 Oct after Macklem (Phase 1 [S20][S21]); Phase 1 scored this as already priced (R2 = 0), so the move is the repricing itself. (b) Oil: Brent at a multi-year high (P2-S3, P2-S4). The 4-week change is +9.5% (TE) or ~+13.6% (P2-S6: $102.75 intraday on 25 Sep vs the $116.72 settle on 23 Oct). That is still a level conflict at the +10% threshold (intraday vs settle), so N1 stays capped at +1. Even at +2 the CAD score would remain −2 (raw −1.58). (c) No US tariff change was verified; the WSJ report could not be opened (fetch failed).

Code: P (pricing moved; already reflected) with F possible. UNRESOLVED CONTRADICTION for the CAD strength of late September and early October, before the Macklem repricing.

C2 · USD — scored +2, realised rank 6 / 8 / 7

H1 observation: USD fell in each of the last two weeks (−0.42%, −0.53%) after five weeks of gains; it was flat-to-down on Thursday's risk-off day.

Explanations checked: December hike odds fell to ~76–80% on 22–23 Oct and the 10-year yield dropped ~17bp on Thursday to ~5% (P2-S1, P2-S2). The Phase 1 report cited ~94% (20 Oct, Norada quoting FedWatch). That is a stale citation of the priced path (code M, documentation only). It does not change R2: communication is neutral (Warsh gave no guidance) and the data trend is mixed (hot CPI/PPI vs September payrolls +29k), so §5.1 keeps R2 at 0 either way. Lower pricing against a unanimous December call in the Reuters poll would, if anything, lean hawkish, not dovish.

Code: P (pricing moved the other way before the baseline). The −0.5 haven beta did not show this week; that is a calibration item, not a correction.

C3 · XAU — G +1 (XAU +3, XAUUSD two-sided), gold −4.1% on the week, −6.7% over four weeks

Code: F (liquidation and positioning, per Phase 1 [S35]) as the working hypothesis. UNRESOLVED CONTRADICTION: gold fell on a risk-off day with lower real yields.

The 4-week weakness comes from the 12–16 Oct jobs week (−1.05%). The completed week reversed it (+0.94%, rank 1). Code P: a known event, then repriced by the RBA's communication. No change.
