1 · How to read this

  • Zone = a price area where the market turned strongly before. We wait for price to come back to it.
  • Confirmation = we do not act when price touches the zone. We wait for a sign that it is turning (best sign: price grabs the stop orders just past a level and closes back).
  • Invalidation = the price where the idea is wrong. A daily close beyond it = forget it.
  • Grade = quality. A best, B good, C weaker. Prefer A and B.

6 · Glossary

ATR
The size of a normal day (or 4-hour bar) move. "1.5 × a day's move" means one and a half normal days.
Buying zone (demand)
A price area where buyers pushed price up strongly before. Price often bounces there again.
Selling zone (supply)
A price area where sellers pushed price down strongly before. Price often turns down there again.
Support / resistance
Prices where the market turned several times. Support is below price, resistance above.
Liquidity / stop orders
Places (often just above old highs or below old lows) where many traders have stop orders. Price is often pulled there, grabs the stops, then reverses.
Sweep
Price goes just past a level, takes the stop orders there, and closes back. Often a sign of a reversal.
FVG (price gap)
A fast move that left a gap in prices. Price often comes back to fill part of it.
Trend
Going up = higher highs and higher lows. Going down = lower highs and lower lows. Sideways = neither.
Upper / lower half (premium / discount)
Where price is inside its 3-month range. The method buys in the lower half and sells in the upper half.
Invalidation
The price where the idea is clearly wrong. A daily close beyond it means: forget the idea.
Possible reward (R:R)
How far the next level is compared with the distance to the invalidation. 2× means the possible gain is twice the possible loss.
Grade A / B / C
A = everything agrees (fundamentals, both charts, a fresh strong zone). B = most things agree. C = weaker idea. This week there is no A.
Technical-only
An idea from the chart alone, without help from fundamentals. Always grade C.
