2Executive summary

The W44 ranking did not carry useful information this week. The Spearman correlation between the published scores and realised currency strength was -0.12 (permutation p = 0.62, one week, insufficient sample). The top-three scored currencies (AUD, USD, JPY) underperformed the bottom three (EUR, CHF, CAD) by -0.12%. PHASE 1 and FINAL are identical (no Phase 2 change), so they score the same.

Pair level was better than the ranking: on directional pairs 7 hits, 3 misses and 8 flat (±0.25σ, uncalibrated). The EUR-short pairs mostly worked (EURUSD, EURGBP, EURCAD, EURCHF, EURAUD), and the single high-conviction pair, EURAUD, was a marginal hit (−0.30%, 0.25σ). Misses: EURNZD, CADJPY, CHFJPY.

What drove the errors was mostly post-cutoff central-bank news: the RBNZ held against ~90% hike pricing (NZD worst, −1.13%); the BoC hiked and signalled more (CAD best, +0.51%, after ranking 2nd the week before, against a −2 score); in-line Australian CPI gave AUD no follow-through. Gold fell 2.96% on a risk-off Friday — the second week running in which the haven assumption failed.

Nothing in the frozen W44 forecast is changed. Lessons are logged as calibration hypotheses; methodology v2.1 is unchanged (1 of 26 required live vintages).

12Calibration observations (hypotheses only)

  • H1 (repeat): "R2 = 0 because a central-bank move is largely priced" left the score blind to the outcome of a binary meeting inside the week (RBNZ ~90% priced → hold; BoC ~80% → hike plus hawkish guidance). Track whether currencies with a CRITICAL decision in the target week show larger rank gaps.
  • H2 (repeat): haven betas — on Friday's risk-off session (S&P −1.82%) JPY gained only +0.10% and CHF lost 0.09%; gold fell on a risk-off day for the second week running. Review betas and X2 at 26 weeks (§8.5).
  • H5 (new): CAD has ranked 1st or 2nd in realised strength in consecutive weeks while scoring −2; the low-carry R1 (−1) and tariff N2 (−1) may be outweighed by oil and BoC repricing. Count occurrences; a methodology proposal needs the same code on the same factor 4 times in 12 weeks (§8.4).
  • Data: the triangular tolerance of 0.05% is tighter than the two-decimal rounding envelope of the export (~0.075% weekly). Suggest the app exports three or four decimals or close levels; no rule change proposed.

16Lessons for future research (no rule change)

  • Event risk inside the target week: for every CRITICAL central-bank decision in the target week, record the priced probability and the call dispersion (e.g. RBNZ: all major banks expected a hike) and state what a surprise in either direction would do to R2/R3 next week.
  • Source checking: open the central bank's own release for every decision used (only secondary sources were reachable this week for the BoC and RBNZ).
  • Timing: refresh priced paths within ~3 trading days of cutoff (repeat of the W44 USD citation lesson).
  • Gold: look for weekly ETF-flow and CFTC data before scoring X3/X5; without them X3 stays grade C.
